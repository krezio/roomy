Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Short version: Roomy doesn't collect, store, or send any of your data. Everything happens on your PC.

What Roomy does on your PC

To find space you can free up, Roomy reads file and folder names, sizes and dates in known cache and temporary locations, your Downloads folder, and your Steam library files. When you use the Space map it reads the sizes of folders on the drive you pick. This information is only shown to you inside the app and is thrown away when you close it.

Roomy only deletes files after you click Clean up, and only the ones you selected.

What Roomy collects

Nothing. Roomy has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no ads and no crash reporting. It doesn't connect to the internet at all.

Share images

If you press Share my result, Roomy creates an image showing how much space you freed, copies it to your clipboard and saves it to your Pictures folder. It is never uploaded anywhere by Roomy; you decide whether to share it.

This website

roomy.krezio.xyz is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages and uses fonts from Google Fonts. To show the current number of GitHub stars, your browser asks the public GitHub API for it. To count visits, the site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies, doesn't fingerprint you and doesn't collect personal information; it only records things like which pages were viewed, the referring site and the country. There are no other analytics or trackers. GitHub and Google may log standard request information (like your IP address) as part of serving the site; see their privacy policies.

Children

Roomy doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
